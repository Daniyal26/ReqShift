SRS-2085  Modular Assembly Cell MAC-300, Control Requirements
Revision A

3.1 Scope

This document specifies the control requirements for the modular assembly cell MAC-300.
The cell is built from a catalogue of modules and is delivered in a different composition
for each customer. One cell controller is always present.

3.2 Cell composition

Work is carried out at assembly modules mounted on the transfer frame. A cell shall carry
at least one and at most four assembly modules.

Parts are placed by handling robots. A robot may be shared between two neighbouring
assembly modules, so the number of robots ordered with a cell is not fixed by this
document.

Finished assemblies are checked at test modules. Test modules are optional on cells that
feed a downstream inspection line.

Operators interact with the cell through operator panels. Panels are ordered per shift
layout.

3.3 Cell controller

The cell controller shall complete an assembly job. A job is complete when every station
step has been carried out, the assembly has been tested and the result has been recorded.

The controller shall treat a station step as carried out only once at least one assembly
module reports that step as finished.

The controller shall treat an assembly as tested only once at least one test module
reports a verdict for it.

The controller shall record a job as released only once an operator has confirmed the
result at an operator panel.

3.4 Assembly modules

Each assembly module shall carry out its configured station step and report completion to
the cell controller.

An assembly module shall not start a step before the part is in position. A part is in
position only once at least one handling robot reports the part as placed.

3.5 Handling robots

Each handling robot shall place a part at the requested module and report the placement.

3.6 Test modules

Each test module shall test an assembly and report a pass or fail verdict to the cell
controller.
