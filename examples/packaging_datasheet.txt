TDS-9310  Horizontal Flow Wrapper HFW-400, Technical Data Sheet
Revision 2

1. Description

The HFW-400 is a horizontal flow wrapping machine for food and non food products. It is
supplied as part of a packaging line together with infeed conveyors, a checkweigher and a
case packer.

2. Performance data

Output rate up to 120 packs per minute for standard products.

Film speed up to 2.5 m/s. Reduced to 1.8 m/s for perforated film.

Product weight up to 60 kg per pack.

Product length between 80 mm and 420 mm.

Sealing temperature adjustable from 80 to 220 degrees Celsius, controlled to within
2 degrees.

Changeover time under 15 minutes for a trained operator.

3. Dimensions and utilities

Machine footprint max. 4200 mm long, max. 1350 mm wide, max. 1800 mm high.

Infeed height adjustable between 850 mm and 1050 mm.

Connected load 8.5 kW. Compressed air 6 bar, up to 180 litres per minute.

Noise emission below 75 dB(A) at one metre.

4. Line integration

The wrapper accepts product from upstream infeed conveyors and discharges to downstream
case packing equipment. Line composition is agreed with the customer during project
planning.

Additional infeed conveyors may be fitted depending on product handling requirements.

The machine communicates with the line control system over Ethernet.

5. Availability

Mean time between failures above 900 operating hours.

Machine availability at least 98 percent under normal production conditions.
