SRS-7720  Bottling Line, Control Requirements
Revision B

Revision B closes the composition gaps left open in Revision A. Every machine kind now
carries a stated minimum and maximum.

2.1 Scope

This document specifies the control behaviour of bottling line BL-2. The line is
supervised by a single line controller, which remains powered whenever the plant is
energised.

2.2 Line composition

The line shall be fitted with at least one and at most three filling heads. Filling heads
are removed for cleaning between product changes, and the line is expected to continue
operating with a reduced complement, but never with fewer than one.

Capping is performed by capping units. The line shall be fitted with at least one and at
most two capping units.

Finished crates are removed by pallet shuttles. At least one and at most two pallet
shuttles shall be assigned to the line at any time.

2.3 Line controller

The line controller shall complete a production run. A run is complete when every bottle
in the run has been filled, capped and palletised.

The controller shall only consider a bottle filled once at least one filling head reports
that bottle as filled.

The controller shall only consider a bottle capped once at least one capping unit reports
that bottle as capped.

The controller shall only consider a crate removed once at least one pallet shuttle
reports the crate as collected.

2.4 Filling heads

Each filling head shall fill bottles to the configured volume and report each completed
bottle to the controller.
