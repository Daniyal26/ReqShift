SRS-4410  Shuttle Warehouse Control System, Requirements
Revision C

1.1 Scope

This document specifies the control system for automated shuttle warehouse SW-7. The
warehouse is supervised by a single warehouse control system, which runs whenever the
site is in operation.

1.2 Warehouse composition

Storage and retrieval within the racking is carried out by rack shuttles. Between one and
four rack shuttles may be assigned to the aisle at any time. Shuttles are taken out of
service for battery exchange and maintenance, and the warehouse is expected to keep
operating on the remaining complement.

Loads are moved between levels by lifts. The aisle is served by at most two lifts.

Shuttles recharge at charging stations installed at the aisle head. Several charging
stations may be fitted depending on the duty cycle required.

Goods leave the system through pick stations. The number of pick stations varies from
site to site.

1.3 Warehouse control system

The warehouse control system shall complete a retrieval order. An order is complete when
every load in the order has been taken from its rack location, carried to the correct
level and presented to an operator.

The control system shall treat a load as retrieved only once at least one rack shuttle
reports that load as taken from its location.

The control system shall treat a load as transferred only once at least one lift reports
that load as delivered to the target level.

The control system shall treat an order as presented only once at least one pick station
reports the load as available to the operator.

1.4 Rack shuttles

Each rack shuttle shall travel to a rack location, take or place a load, and report the
movement to the control system.

A rack shuttle shall report its state of charge. A shuttle shall only resume work after a
charging cycle once at least one charging station reports the shuttle as released.

1.5 Lifts

Each lift shall carry a load to the requested level and report the transfer to the
control system.

1.6 Pick stations

Each pick station shall present a load to an operator and confirm the pick to the control
system.
