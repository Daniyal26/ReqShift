FDP-1000  Food Delivery Platform, Operational Requirements
Revision A

1. Scope

This document specifies the runtime behaviour of a multi-sided food delivery platform
connecting customers, restaurant partners, delivery riders and a central backend. It
covers only what must hold while orders are being served; onboarding, admin tooling and
reporting are out of scope here.

2. Participants

The platform backend is a single always-on service. It remains available whenever the
system is running.

Payments are handled by a single external payment provider.

Restaurant partners join and leave the platform continuously. A city may be served by up
to three restaurant partners at any one time. Restaurants close outside business hours
and may pause orders, so the platform is expected to keep operating with a reduced set of
active restaurants.

Delivery riders go online and offline at will. Up to five riders may be online in a city
at any one time.

Up to three customers may have an order in progress at any one time.

3. Platform backend

The backend shall take an order from placement through to delivery. This requires that
the order is routed to a restaurant, that payment is settled, and that the delivery is
tracked in real time.

The backend can only route an order once at least one restaurant partner is able to
fulfil it.

The backend can only track a delivery once at least one rider is able to complete it.

Payment settlement requires the payment provider to authorize the charge.

4. Payment provider

The payment provider shall authorize and capture the charge. This requires both the
fraud and 3-D Secure checks and the capture of funds.

5. Restaurant partners

A restaurant partner shall fulfil an accepted order. Fulfilment requires accepting the
order with a stated preparation time, preparing the food and marking it ready, and
handing the order over for pickup.

6. Delivery riders

A rider shall complete a delivery. A delivery consists of picking the order up at the
restaurant and dropping it at the customer.

A rider can only pick an order up once at least one restaurant partner has fulfilled it.

Drop-off is confirmed either with a delivery code from the customer or with photo proof,
but never both for the same order.

7. Customers

A customer shall receive the food they ordered. This requires placing an order, paying
for it, and receiving the delivery.

A customer can only discover a restaurant and place an order when at least one restaurant
partner is able to fulfil it.

A customer pays either online by card or wallet, or in cash on delivery, but never both
for the same order.

Online payment requires the payment provider to authorize the charge.

The customer receives the food once at least one rider has completed the delivery.

8. Availability

The platform shall tolerate restaurants closing and riders going offline without
requiring an operator restart.
