TS-1180  Depot Charging Site, System Requirements
Revision 2

1. Scope

This document specifies the charging site installed at a bus depot. The site is managed
by one site controller, which is available whenever the depot has power.

2. Site composition

Vehicles are charged at charge points. A depot shall be equipped with at least two and at
most six charge points.

Charge points draw power through transformers. The site is fed by up to two transformers.

Energy readings are collected by meters. A meter is fitted per feeder, and the feeder
layout is decided during site design.

Drivers identify themselves at authentication terminals before a charging session starts.
Terminals are installed according to the depot layout.

3. Site controller

The site controller shall deliver a completed charging session. A session is complete
when the vehicle has been authenticated, energy has been delivered and the delivered
energy has been recorded.

The site controller shall treat a vehicle as authenticated only once at least one
authentication terminal confirms the driver.

The site controller shall treat energy as delivered only once at least one charge point
reports the session as finished.

The site controller shall treat a session as recorded only once at least one meter
reports the delivered energy for that session.

4. Charge points

Each charge point shall deliver energy to a connected vehicle and report the session to
the site controller.

A charge point shall only start a session while supply is available. Supply is available
only while at least one transformer reports the feeder as energised.

5. Transformers

Each transformer shall supply its feeder and report its loading to the site controller.

6. Meters

Each meter shall measure delivered energy on its feeder and report it to the site
controller.
