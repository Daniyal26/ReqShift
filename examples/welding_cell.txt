SRS-4100  Robotic Welding Cell, Control Requirements
Revision C

4.1 Scope

This document specifies the control behaviour of welding cell WC-3 on the body-in-white
line. The cell is supervised by a single cell controller, which remains powered
whenever the line is energised.

4.2 Cell composition

The cell may operate with up to four welding robots. Robots are removed from the cell
for scheduled maintenance and for retooling between product variants, and the cell is
expected to continue operating with a reduced complement.

Part transport into and out of the cell is performed by automated guided vehicles. Up to
three vehicles may be assigned to the cell at any one time. Vehicles are dispatched from
a shared fleet and may be reassigned to other cells by the plant scheduler.

4.3 Cell controller

The cell controller shall complete a production batch. A batch is complete when the
controller has scheduled all jobs in the batch and has received every welded part back
from the cell.

The controller schedules jobs from the batch queue without assistance from other
equipment in the cell.

The controller shall only consider a batch part complete once at least one welding robot
reports that part as welded.

4.4 Welding robots

A welding robot shall weld its assigned part. To do so the robot must first receive the
raw part at its station, and must then run the weld program for the current variant.

A robot may run either the standard weld program or the high-strength weld program for a
given part, but never both on the same part.

A robot can only receive its assigned part once at least one transport vehicle has
delivered raw stock to the cell.

4.5 Transport vehicles

A transport vehicle shall deliver parts to the cell. Delivery consists of collecting
stock from the staging area and moving it to the cell infeed buffer. Both steps are
required for a delivery to count as complete.

Vehicles operate independently of the welding robots and of each other.

4.6 Availability

The cell shall tolerate the loss of individual robots and vehicles without requiring an
operator restart.
