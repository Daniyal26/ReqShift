SPEC-2210  Mobile Dialysis Unit, Coordination Requirements
Revision A

2.1 Scope

This document specifies coordination within a mobile dialysis service operating across a
rural district. All times in this document are given in minutes.

2.2 Participants

The service is directed by a single scheduling office, which is staffed at all times.

The district operates up to eight treatment vans. Vans are withdrawn for servicing and
for driver rest periods, and the service is expected to continue with the vans that
remain available.

Treatment is delivered at up to three clinic sites. A site may close to new admissions
when its water treatment plant is under maintenance.

2.3 Scheduling office

The scheduling office shall complete the daily treatment round. It does this by
assigning patients to vans and by confirming that every assigned patient has been
treated. Assigning patients takes 6 minutes. Confirming the round takes 4 minutes.

The office can only confirm the round once at least one treatment van reports its
patients as treated.

The scheduling office shall complete the daily round within 120 minutes.

2.4 Treatment vans

A treatment van shall treat its assigned patients. To do so the van must first travel to
a clinic site, and must then run the dialysis session.

A van reaches a site either by the main road or by the forest track, but never both on
one journey. Travel by main road takes 22 minutes. Travel by the forest track takes 35
minutes.

Running a dialysis session takes 45 minutes.

A van can only run its session once a clinic site has released a treatment bay to it.

2.5 Clinic sites

A clinic site shall support the day's treatments. Supporting treatments means releasing
treatment bays and logging the water quality result. Releasing a bay takes 8 minutes.

Each clinic site has four treatment bays. Each treatment van occupies one bay for the
duration of its session.
